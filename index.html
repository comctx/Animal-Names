About this preview

This is a working design prototype with 20 animal profiles. Names, diet and habitat entries are editorial drafts. Numerical facts and conservation assessments are included only where noted; several sections still need research. Traditional collective names are expressions, not scientific terms.

Real, properly licensed species photographs are being added animal by animal. Entries without a photo still use a temporary animal symbol. The giraffe entry currently covers a genus rather than one species.

Browsing and searching use the information in this file, with no internet needed. Source links need a connection. Favorites save on this browser when storage is available. “Hear name” uses your device’s voice, which may require an internet connection. It is not a recording of the animal.

No accounts, analytics, advertising or purchases are included. This preview is not yet an installable iPhone app.
